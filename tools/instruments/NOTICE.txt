Blaze retains the shared trunk and six independent, unmodified mask heads:
acoustic-guitar (1), electric-guitar (16), guitar (20), strings (37), brass (8),
synth (38). Other unused heads are omitted without changing retained parameters.
Full author-recommended 882000-sample context (20 seconds), 44.1 kHz stereo,
periodic Hann FFT2048/hop512, FP32 inference. Components run sequentially.

Guitar evidence is max-pooled across three heads, not summed. Instrument-family
energy scores produce bounded, nonnegative spectral masks; unexplained energy
stays in Other. The original phase is retained. This application projection is
distinct from the raw complex-valued model outputs and their published SDRs.
Conversion parity validates the raw selected heads, not new musical accuracy.

This is the published open model, not MVSep's newer hosted service model.
Bleed, missed notes and quiet estimates for absent instruments remain possible.
