This Android app includes a persistent queue/history, video quality selection, original audio/MP3/M4A/WAV/FLAC, separate-stream merging, and bounded playlists. Pause/resume retains supported partial files; interrupted work remains in the queue. Save a copy to keep media outside Blaze before uninstalling. Android can stop or time-limit background services. Live streams, login/cookies and the desktop dashboard are not included. Some sites block downloading or change independently. Download only content you are allowed to save. WAV/FLAC conversion cannot restore detail missing from the source.

The offline chord finder analyses local audio/video soundtracks on-device and saves timestamped chord estimates. No microphone, server, upload, or model download is used. Chord/key accuracy on full mixes is not established; A legacy basic analyser is retained separately; Music Studio defaults to the pretrained extended model described below.

Music Studio uses pretrained BTC-170 checkpoints, exported to ONNX without training. BTC source and checkpoints: MIT, copyright 2019 Jonggwon Park, https://github.com/jayg996/BTC-ISMIR19 at 2682317be668032e6e4b269ded36adaa2ad57df0. Full model license and checksums are in assets/musicstudio_models.
ONNX Runtime Android 1.20.0: MIT, copyright Microsoft Corporation, https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime. Recognition stays local; no microphone permission or cloud inference. The default recognises major, minor, diminished, augmented, minor/major sixth, minor/minor-major/major/dominant/diminished/half-diminished seventh, sus2 and sus4; ninths, elevenths and thirteenths can be entered manually but are not automatically detected. N = no chord, X = uncertain. Model scores are uncalibrated; song accuracy has not been benchmarked. Spectral-flux beat timestamps and tempo are estimates. Meter and downbeats require manual confirmation; without it, Live Chord Flow displays neutral timing windows. Rhythm corrections persist separately from the original analysis.
