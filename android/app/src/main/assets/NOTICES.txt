Free to install and use. Other rights reserved under the included BLAZE-LICENSE.txt. Your downloads stay on your device. There is no Blaze account, analytics or server. Requests go directly to the website you choose.

This Android app includes a persistent queue/history, video quality selection, original audio/MP3/M4A/WAV/FLAC, separate-stream merging, and bounded playlists. Pause/resume retains supported partial files; interrupted work remains in the queue. Save a copy to keep media outside Blaze before uninstalling. Android can stop or time-limit background services. Live streams, login/cookies and the desktop dashboard are not included. Some sites block downloading or change independently. Download only content you are allowed to save. WAV/FLAC conversion cannot restore detail missing from the source.
